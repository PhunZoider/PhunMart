# Machine art prompts

Image-generation prompts for the shop textures. Attach the layout reference with each prompt.

Each prompt makes four panels. They land on the texture like this:

| Panel          | Lands on              | Texture size | Ratio  |
| -------------- | --------------------- | ------------ | ------ |
| Top banner     | Banner + shop window  | 457 x 154    | ~3:1   |
| Bottom banner  | Spare plate (no text) |              | ~3:1   |
| Left vertical  | Machine's left side   | 142 x 467    | ~1:3.3 |
| Right vertical | Machine's right side  | 142 x 467    | ~1:3.3 |

The left side panel's left edge faces the back of the machine, so subjects on the left panel face
right and subjects on the right panel face left. That way both sides look toward the front.

First pass done: budget-xp, car-a-part, collectors, csv, gifted-xp, good-phoods, luxury-xp. Their restyles
are under [Restyles of the finished shops](#restyles-of-the-finished-shops).

---

## Template

```
Flat-lay design sheet: four separate print panels on a plain white background, seen straight on, with wide white gaps between them. No perspective, no mockup, no frames, borders or drop shadows, nothing outside the panels. Layout as in the reference image: on the left, two wide horizontal banners stacked, each about 3:1 (width to height); on the right, two tall narrow vertical panels side by side, each about 1:3.3 and as tall as the whole left column.

Top banner (main sign): the store name "[NAME]" in large, bold, legible lettering, centred, with wide margins on every side. [LOGO]. Under the name, the tagline "[TAGLINE]" in smaller lettering. Behind it: [SCENE].

Bottom banner: the same scene and style as the top banner, with no lettering or logo at all. A clean background plate.

Vertical panels: extensions of the same brand, with no text and no logos. Each is filled edge to edge with [PANELS]. Keep the main subject in the middle third, since the top and bottom may be cropped. The left panel's subject faces right and the right panel's subject faces left, so the two read as a matched pair.

Style: hand-painted video game texture art in the style of Project Zomboid, 1990s rural Kentucky. Painterly, simplified shapes with visible brush strokes, slightly grimy and worn, soft flat lighting, no glossy highlights, no photographic detail, no depth-of-field blur. Muted, slightly desaturated colours. Lettering stays crisp and readable. Theme flavour: [STYLE]. Palette: [PALETTE]. Rich mid-tones; avoid large areas of pure white or pure black. Spell all lettering exactly as quoted. Original artwork, not based on any real brand's logo.
```
